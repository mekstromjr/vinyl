What do the grades mean?
Mint — looks unplayed. Crisp corners, glossy jacket, no shelf or ring wear at all. Rare in a played collection.
Excellent — very light wear: a soft corner, a faint shelf line. You have to look for the flaws to see them.
Good — regular wear for its age: visible ring wear, rounded corners, general handling. The default for this collection.
Fair — not great: writing or drawing on the jacket or label, a seam split, water spotting, noticeable damage.
Poor — everything else: barely holding together, repaired with tape, missing chunks, mold. Grade it and move on.

Grade the set overall — when the jacket and discs disagree badly, pick the worse one and say why in the notes. When stuck between two grades, take the lower.
